New `Section` primitive: a band of a page with an edge-to-edge background, space above and below from the brand's spacing roles, and content centred at one of the brand's container widths (`text`, `media`, `wide`, `page`, `full`) with the page gutter. `tone="subtle"` sets a band apart on the secondary surface; for a dark band, wrap it in ThemeScope.
