New `AspectRatio` primitive: keeps an image, video, picture or embed at a fixed ratio (`1/1`, `4/3`, `3/2`, `16/9`, `21/9`, `3/4`, `9/16`, or any number) as the width changes, and holds the space on the secondary surface before the media loads, so the page doesn't jump. `fit` chooses `cover` or `contain`; `rounded` uses the brand's image radius.
