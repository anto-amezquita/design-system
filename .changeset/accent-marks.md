Lines and marks that show a state use `color-accent-text` instead of the accent fill: Radio's checked ring, dot and hover border, the active Tabs indicator, Card's featured border, Checkbox's hover border and Table's sort arrows. Unchanged in every brand where the two are equal (base, portfolio, demo-cool, demo-warm); in a pastel brand like demo-soft they now meet 3:1 against the surface (1.83:1 before).
