New `IconButton` primitive: an action shown only as an icon. It's built on Button, so variants, focus, `loading` and `disabled` behave the same; `ghost` is the default. `label` is required and becomes the accessible name. `md` is a 44px square; `sm` draws a 28px square with a 44px hit area. No new tokens.
