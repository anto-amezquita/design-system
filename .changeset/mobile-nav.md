New `MobileNav` pattern: the bar at the bottom of a small screen with three to five main places, each an icon with a label that's always shown. The current one has `aria-current="page"` and a filled pill. Badges show a count and read it after the label ("Messages (3 unread)"). The bar is at least 44px tall per link, clears the phone's home indicator, and hides from 1024px up unless `alwaysVisible`.
