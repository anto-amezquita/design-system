New `Divider` primitive: a horizontal or vertical line between groups of content. It renders `role="separator"` (with `aria-orientation` when vertical), or `role="none"` with `decorative`. It uses `color-border-default` and `border-width-default`, so demo-warm's divider is 2px like its other borders, and it's drawn as a border so it stays visible in forced-colors mode.
