New `Toolbar` pattern (+ `ToolbarSeparator`, `ToolbarSpacer`): the WAI-ARIA toolbar. The whole row is one Tab stop, and the arrow keys move between controls, wrapping at the ends and skipping disabled ones. Home and End jump to the first and last control, and Tab back in returns to the control last used. Text fields keep their arrow keys, and so does a Menu opened from the toolbar. Horizontal or vertical.
