New `Lightbox` composition (+ `LightboxGallery`): images large, one at a time, in a modal on the brand's own surface. Previous and next buttons, the arrow keys, Home and End, and a sideways swipe move through the images, wrapping at the ends. Each change is announced. Escape or Close returns focus to what opened it, such as a thumbnail. The dialog is named with the position ("Places 2 of 4"), and `labels` translate the controls.
