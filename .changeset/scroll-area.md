New `ScrollArea` primitive: a box that scrolls vertically, horizontally or both, with the browser's own scrollbars styled thin in the strong border colour. It's a named, focusable region (`label` is required), so keyboard users can scroll it even when nothing inside takes focus, and `overscroll-behavior: contain` keeps the page still at its ends. No JavaScript scrollbars.
