New `ProgressBar` primitive: how far a task has got, or an indeterminate bar while the end isn't known. It's a `role="progressbar"` named by its `label`, with optional `showValue` and `valueText` ("3 of 10 files"). The fill uses `color-accent-text`, which two new contrast pairs keep 3:1 against the track and the page in every brand and mode. Under reduced motion the indeterminate segment stays still. Adds one component token, `progress-bar-height`.
