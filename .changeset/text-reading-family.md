`Text` takes a `family`: `base` (default), `mono`, or `reading` for long-form text people read rather than scan, like lyrics, articles and quoted passages. `reading` uses a new semantic token, `font-family-reading`, which is the base font in base, so it looks the same as `base` until a brand sets its own. A brand may set it: it's in `docs/brand-contract.md`. Nothing changes for text that doesn't ask for it.
