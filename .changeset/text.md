New `Text` primitive: body text in the brand's type roles (`body`, `small`, `caption`), with `tone` (`primary`, `secondary`, `inherit`), `weight` (`body`, `label`), and `truncate` to one line or a number of lines while the full text stays in the page. `as` picks the element (`p` by default, `span`, `strong`, `em`, and more). Primary and secondary text are tested at 4.5:1 on all three page surfaces in every brand and mode.
