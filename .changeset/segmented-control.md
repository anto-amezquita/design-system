New `SegmentedControl` primitive: one choice from a few, side by side. It's a Radix radio group, so arrow keys move and choose and Tab treats it as one stop. The chosen segment is filled with the accent (4.5:1 text in every brand and mode), the outline uses `color-border-strong`, and the shape follows the brand's Button radius. Supports icons, icon-only (`hideLabels`), `fullWidth`, and per-option `disabled`. No new tokens.
