New `Icon` primitive: a Phosphor icon (`icon={BellIcon}`) at the brand's icon sizes (`xs`–`xl`) and colours (`current`, `primary`, `secondary`, `accent`, `error`), with Phosphor's `weight`. It's hidden from screen readers by default; with `label` it reads as an image with that name. Every tone is tested at 3:1 on the page surfaces in all five brands and modes.
