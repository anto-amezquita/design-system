New `Item` primitive: one row in a List, with `leading` media, a `title` and `description`, and `trailing` metadata or actions. With `href` the whole row is one link named by its title, while trailing buttons stay their own targets; `current` marks the page you're on with `aria-current` and a bar in the brand's accent.
