New `SelectableCard` primitive, with `SelectableCardGroup`: choices shown as cards, one (`type="single"`, native radios) or several (`multiple`, native checkboxes), inside a `fieldset` whose `legend` is the question. Each card has a `title`, optional `meta` and `description`; the whole card is clickable, arrow keys and forms work as usual, and checked shows as a doubled accent edge and a filled indicator. `columns` lays cards side by side through Grid.
