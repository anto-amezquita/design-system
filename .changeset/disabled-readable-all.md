Disabled controls stay readable. Button, Checkbox, Radio, Switch, Field, Input, Textarea, OtpInput, Select, Slider, Accordion, Tabs and Pagination no longer fade to the 0.1 disabled opacity; disabled now uses a quiet surface and border with secondary text (4.5:1 in every brand, light and dark). A loading Button keeps its variant's colours. `--opacity-disabled` is no longer used by any component.
